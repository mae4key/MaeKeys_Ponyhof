About

MaeKeys Ponyhof is a small, free, self-hosted community project keeping classic early-2000s multiplayer games like Battlefield alive — because the official matchmaking services for these games have long been shut down. Everything here runs on a home server, queried directly and shown live, with no third-party listing service in between.

Currently hosting Battlefield 1942, Battlefield Vietnam, Battlefield 2, Battlefield 2142, Medal of Honor: Allied Assault (via the open-source OpenMoHAA project) and Medal of Honor: Pacific Assault, including a couple of classic mods alongside vanilla. On the roadmap, as longer-term, more ambitious goals: Battlefield: Bad Company 2 and Medal of Honor: Airborne.

Available now: start or configure a server yourself from our Discord – see “Start your own server” below. Servers stop automatically when they are empty.

How it works (Discord)
  1. Join our Discord and get the Member role.
  2. Open the channel with the pinned message “Game servers” and press Start server (default settings) or Configure server (your own settings). Typing /start or /config works too.
  3. Choose a server from the list. Only servers that are free right now are offered.
  4. Configure: go through the pages with Back and Next – game mode, map size, start map, rounds, time limit, players and bots, and more. Everything you leave on “Default” stays as it is. The map list only shows maps of the chosen size.
  5. Press Apply & restart (or Start server). After about a minute the server shows up here on the status page and in the in-game server browser, named after you.

Good to know:

  • The servers are hosted on a home PC. If it is switched off, starting is not possible – see the operating hours on the Servers page.
  • A server with players on it cannot be changed or restarted.
  • Servers started from Discord have no password.
  • Empty servers shut down automatically; your settings are used for that run only.

    How the ranking works
    • Points are score gains of human players on the internet servers. Bots and LAN games are not counted.
    • Players are ranked by their exact nickname. The same nickname is added up across all games in the Overall ranking; a different nickname counts as a separate entry.
    • Nicknames starting with "Player" (the default name) are not shown.
    • A gaming day ends at 04:00 (CET/CEST), so late sessions still count for the day before. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
    • The current period is an interim standing and is updated about every 15 minutes.

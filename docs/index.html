About

MaeKeys Ponyhof is a small, free, self-hosted community project keeping classic early-2000s multiplayer games like Battlefield alive — because the official matchmaking services for these games have long been shut down. Everything here runs on a home server, queried directly and shown live, with no third-party listing service in between.

Currently hosting Battlefield 1942, Battlefield Vietnam, Battlefield 2 and Battlefield 2142, including a couple of classic mods alongside vanilla. On the roadmap: Medal of Honor: Allied Assault (via the open-source OpenMoHAA project) and Medal of Honor: Pacific Assault — and, as a longer-term, more ambitious goal, Battlefield: Bad Company 2.

    How the ranking works
    • Points are score gains of human players on the internet servers. Bots and LAN games are not counted.
    • Players are ranked by their exact nickname. The same nickname is added up across all games in the Overall ranking; a different nickname counts as a separate entry.
    • Nicknames starting with "Player" (the default name) are not shown.
    • A gaming day ends at 04:00 (CET/CEST), so late sessions still count for the day before. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
    • The current period is an interim standing and is updated about every 15 minutes.
